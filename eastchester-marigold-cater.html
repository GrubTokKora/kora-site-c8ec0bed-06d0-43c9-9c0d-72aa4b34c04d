Catering questions

Good to know before you book

Hosting a party at the restaurant instead? See private parties.

What catering services do you offer?

Full-service catering with setup, serving, clean-up and staff, or drop-off catering with fresh, carefully packed dishes ready to serve. Pick-up can also be arranged.

Can you cater for dietary restrictions?

Yes. We accommodate vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests and can customize portion sizes to your guest count.

What is on the catering menu?

Starters and appetizers such as samosas and pakoras, main courses including curries, biryanis, tandoori items and vegetarian specialties, breads, rice, sides and sauces, plus desserts and sweets.

What do you need to know to send a quote?

Your event type and date, venue location, number of guests, preferred service style (buffet, plated or full-service) and any special dietary or culinary requests.

How do I get a quote?

Send the catering inquiry form, call (347) 400-5094 or email info@marigold.restaurant. We will follow up with sample menus, pricing estimates and a detailed quote.
