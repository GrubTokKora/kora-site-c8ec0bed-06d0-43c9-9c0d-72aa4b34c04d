Good to know

Reservation questions

Can't find your answer? Call (914) 202-9455 or email info@marigold.restaurant.

Do I need a reservation?

We recommend booking ahead, especially for weekend dinners. Coming without a booking? Call (914) 202-9455 and we will tell you how busy we are.

How do I book a table?

Reserve online through Resy or Yelp, or call us at (914) 202-9455 and we will take care of it.

Can I book for a large group?

For parties of six or more, or to reserve a semi-private section or the whole dining room, call our events line at (347) 400-5094.

How do I change or cancel my reservation?

Use the link in your Resy or Yelp confirmation, or call us at (914) 202-9455.

Can I let you know about allergies or a special occasion?

Yes. Add a note to your booking or tell us when you call, and let your server know when you arrive.

When can I book?

Monday to Thursday we seat guests from 11:30 AM to 3 PM and again from 4:30 to 10 PM. Friday and Saturday we are open 11:30 AM to 10 PM, and Sunday until 9:30 PM.
