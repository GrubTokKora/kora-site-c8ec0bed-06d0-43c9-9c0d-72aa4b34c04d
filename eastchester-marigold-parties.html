Party questions

Planning a party at Marigold

Talk to our events team at (347) 400-5094 or info@marigold.restaurant.

Can you accommodate dietary needs?

Yes. We customize party menus for vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free guests and other dietary needs.

Do you offer drinks for parties?

Yes. Full beverage service is available, including cocktails, wine and beer.

Can we set up music or a presentation?

Optional entertainment and AV setup can be arranged if your event needs it; just mention it in your inquiry.

Can we taste the menu first?

Menu tastings and fully custom menus are available on request.

How quickly will you reply?

We respond promptly with availability, package details and a personalized proposal.
