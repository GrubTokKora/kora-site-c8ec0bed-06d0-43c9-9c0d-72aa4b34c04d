Welcome to Marigold

Neighborhood spot for good food and moments worth sharing.

At Marigold, we celebrate the rich tapestry of innovation while honoring timeless traditions. Founded on the belief that Indian food should feel both genuine and welcoming, our restaurant brings together the warmth of tradition with the creativity of innovation.

Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi leads the way with years of experience and a deep passion for Indian cooking. Every dish is prepared with the finest ingredients and carefully balanced spices, offering tastes that are both classic and contemporary. From cherished family recipes to inventive new creations, each plate is crafted with care to delight and inspire.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Something else on your mind? Call us at (914) 202-9455 or write to info@marigold.restaurant.

Do I need a reservation?

We recommend booking ahead, especially on weekends. Reserve on Resy or Yelp, or call (914) 202-9455.

When is happy hour?

Monday to Thursday from 4:30 to 7 PM. Selected appetizers are $7, and cocktails, beer and wine are half price.

Do you have vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options?

Yes. Our menu has a full vegetarian section and many dishes can be made vegan or gluten-free. Let your server know about any dietary needs or allergies.

Can I order takeout or delivery?

Yes. Order online at ordermarigoldrestaurant.com for pickup or delivery, or call us to place a pickup order.

Do you cater events and host private parties?

We offer full-service and drop-off catering, and host groups from a semi-private section up to the entire dining room. Use the inquiry form above or call our events line at (347) 400-5094.

Is there a lunch menu?

Yes. Choose one entrée, served with rice, dal and naan, from $15. On weekends, our early bird special is three courses with a champagne toast for $40, from 11:30 AM to 3 PM.
