Our commitment

Everyone should be able to read our menu, book a table, plan an event or find their way to 434 White Plains Road. Marigold is committed to making this website accessible to people with disabilities, and we keep improving it based on the feedback we receive.

Conformance

To provide a great experience to everyone, we use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). The guidelines have three levels of accessibility: A, AA and AAA. Marigold has chosen level AA as the target for this website, and we are committed to monitoring the site to maintain this level.

On this website

Some of the ways this site is built to work for more people:

  • Skip link: a “Skip to main content” link is the first thing on every page.
  • Keyboard access: menus, tabs, the reservation menu, FAQs and photo viewers work with a keyboard, with visible focus.
  • Text alternatives: meaningful images have descriptive alt text; decorative ones are hidden from screen readers.
  • Clear structure: one main heading per page, ordered headings and labelled page regions.
  • Video controls: the homepage video starts muted and can be paused or unmuted at any time.
  • Reduced motion: animations are switched off when your device asks for reduced motion.
  • Accessible forms: every field has a label, and errors are described in text next to the field.
  • Readable on any screen: pages reflow for phones and tablets and support browser zoom.

Third-party content

Some features are provided by partners, including online ordering, reservations on Resy and Yelp, social media, review sites and Google Maps. We encourage these vendors to provide content that is accessible and user friendly, even though we cannot control the content they show on our website.
