About Marigold and Chef Ravi, Eastchester NY

Our Story

Meet the chef

Chef Ravi, bringing authentic Indian cuisine to your table.

Led by Chef-Entrepreneur Ravi, Marigold blends tradition with innovation, creating dishes that honor age-old recipes while inviting new tastes to the table.

Chef Ravi grew up immersed in the aromas, spices and stories of Indian kitchens. After years of training with masters, traveling across India and refining his craft, he set out to bring that heritage to Eastchester, with a twist.

RaviChef-Entrepreneur, Marigold

Our story & inspiration

North, South, East and West, on one menu.

At Marigold you will taste recipes rooted in classic regional flavors, each dish carefully composed to balance spice, texture and richness. A few to start with:

  • North

    Goat Rogan Josh

    Kashmiri-style goat curry, a classic of the north.

  • South

    Chettinad Chicken

    Coconut milk and freshly ground spices from Tamil Nadu.

  • East

    Chili Chicken

    The Indo-Chinese favorite from Kolkata, with peppers and chili-garlic sauce.

  • West

    Lamb Vindaloo

    Goan-style curry with tomato, whole red chili and vinegar.

What makes Marigold special

Four promises we keep, every service.

  • 01

    Authentic ingredients & artful cooking

    Fresh, high-quality ingredients and traditional techniques, from the tandoor to slow braises and hand-rolled breads, to bring out bold flavors.

  • 02

    A modern, welcoming ambience

    A room designed to feel warm and intimate, right for a romantic dinner, a family gathering or a night out with friends.

  • 03

    Flexible & inclusive dining

    Vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free or other needs, we have options that delight, plus full bar service, wine pairings and curated desserts.

  • 04

    Community & exceptional hospitality

    Being part of Eastchester means a lot to us. Friendly staff, prompt service and a place where people feel valued.

Our mission

To bring the flavors of India to your table with honesty, care and creativity.

We aim to be more than just a restaurant. For us, Marigold is a place where memories are made and the love for food is shared.
