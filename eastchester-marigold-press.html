Press

Forbes

By John Mariani · December 30, 2024

Marigold named “one of the best in the tri-state region”

Forbes food and travel writer John Mariani reviewed Marigold a year after it opened. He found a concise menu where every dish is distinct, with its own spices and textures, instead of the one-sauce-fits-all cooking found at many suburban Indian restaurants.

He praised the house-made samosas, the pani puri shots, vegetable dishes that taste fresh rather than smothered, and a dining room free of the usual decor clichés.
